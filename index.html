How I work

I write every system to these four rules, so your game stays hard to exploit, easy to expand and fair to play.

01

Server-authoritative

The client asks and the server checks. Placement, upgrades, chests, rewards and saving never trust the client.

02

Data-driven

Towers, weapons, enemies, chests and accessories live in shared data modules. Adding content doesn't mean rewriting code.

03

Structured

One module per job, shared modules identical across lobby and game, and comments that explain why.

04

Balanced with math

Formula-based models connect DPS to power to price, and set enemy HP per wave.

Rogue Defense

Tower DefenseRogueliteLoot SystemMulti-place

Deploy characters, equip weapons from your inventory and survive 50 waves across biomes. Bosses use abilities, elements react with each other, and you pick power-up cards between waves. Weapons drop from chests with rarities, levels and random modifiers.

Game systems
  • Virtual enemies: the server stores only position, health and waypoint, and clients render them
  • Wave generator with an HP budget that scales by wave1.3
  • Difficulties, biomes, portals and party scaling
  • Boss abilities: summon minions, disable towers, corrupt enemies, scramble targeting
  • Server-validated placement with a live range and hitbox preview
  • 7 targeting modes: First, Last, Near, Strong, Weak, Fastest, Slowest
  • 19 weapons built from attack patterns (Single, Splash, Cone, Chain, Burst) on one damage pipeline
  • Data-driven VFX, and burn, chill, poison and charge elements
  • 16 characters with perk modules, plus summoner units
Lobby & progression
  • Weapon inventory with rarities, levels, quality rolls and prefix/suffix modifiers
  • Chest opening with weighted rarity tables
  • Reforging: reroll, amplify or remove modifiers
  • Level-locked unlocks (rarities at 11, reforging at 21, Chaotic gear at 31)
  • One data module shared by both places, with save migration
  • Levels/XP, achievements that unlock accessories, global and weekly leaderboards
  • Party lobbies, and a cafeteria where characters sit as NPCs wearing the player's avatar

Build a Cow

Card GameMatchmakingRanked EloMonetizationMobile-first

A multiplayer card game for 2–6 players. Pair Head and Tail cards to build cows, then steal, scare and swipe each other's herds with action cards and coin-flip challenges. The biggest herd wins.

Game systems
  • The server owns the deck, hands and herds
  • Coin-flip challenges with a Re-Flip interrupt window and spectator betting
  • Bots fill empty seats and take over for AFK players
  • Cross-server Quick Play through a MemoryStore queue, plus friend lobbies with invites
  • Ranked mode (2–4 players) with Elo and tiers, and rejoining after a disconnect
  • Scripted tutorial with a coach character
Meta & monetization
  • Session-locked saves, a 2-minute autosave, and purchase receipts processed exactly once
  • Barn idle system: offline coin jug, barn visits and a barn leaderboard
  • Cow-Dex with 21 entries and a 1-in-50 shiny variant
  • Levels/XP, daily quests, login streaks, badge achievements, and a free + premium season pass
  • Cosmetics from a daily shop, chests, the season pass, bundles and group rewards
  • Developer products, game passes (VIP, 2x coins), gifting, group-join rewards and timed offers
